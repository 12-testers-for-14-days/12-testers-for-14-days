THE REQUIREMENT

What is Google Play’s 12 testers for 14 days rule?

The 12 testers for 14 days rule is a Google Play requirement for new personal developer accounts. Accounts created after November 13, 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers who stay opted in for 14 continuous days before applying for production access.

Google originally asked for 20 testers and lowered the minimum to 12 in December 2024, while the 14-day period stayed the same. Reaching 12 testers only unlocks the production application. Google still reviews your answers about testing, feedback and readiness.

  • 12 testers minimum, opted in continuously
  • 14 continuous days of closed testing
  • Nov 13, 2023 is the account-creation cutoff

Source: App testing requirements for new personal developer accounts (Play Console Help).

Frequently asked questions: requirements and safety

1. What is the Google Play 14-day rule?

Google requires all new personal developer accounts created after Nov 13, 2023, to run a closed test with at least 12 independent testers who must stay opted-in for 14 continuous days before you can apply for production access.

2. Are your testers real humans or bots?

We strictly use 100% real human testers using physical Android devices. Google's algorithm easily detects emulators and bot farms, which leads to immediate account bans. We never use bots.

3. Do testers actually open the app every day?

Yes. Google monitors active engagement metrics. Our team interacts with your app daily, navigating screens to generate healthy session data, ensuring you don't get rejected for "insufficient testing".

4. Is this allowed by Google Play Policy?

Absolutely. Google asks developers to find 12 independent users to test their app. You are simply hiring a QA testing agency (us) to fulfill this requirement professionally.

5. What if my app gets rejected for "Insufficient Testing"?

This is very rare with our service. However, if it happens, we offer a 100% Free Retest Guarantee. We will test your app for another 14 days at absolutely no extra cost.

6. Can you test apps with In-App Purchases (IAP)?

Yes. If your app relies on Google Play Billing, our testers can utilize Promo Codes provided by you in the Play Console to test premium features.

Frequently asked questions: updates, testers and delivery

7. Do I need to publish an update during the 14 days?

It is highly recommended! Pushing 1-2 minor updates during the testing phase shows Google that you are actively responding to feedback. Our testers will seamlessly update to the new version.

8. What happens if a tester uninstalls the app?

Because we manage the testing team professionally, uninstalls do not happen. In the rare event of a device failure, we immediately assign a backup tester to maintain your required streak.

9. Do you provide feedback for the Google questionnaire?

Yes. We provide a summary of the qualitative feedback our testers generated, along with a guide on how to answer Google's questions regarding "what feedback you received and how you acted on it."

10. Can I use my own friends alongside your testers?

Yes! You can add up to 100 testers to your closed test track. Our testers guarantee the baseline requirement, and any friends you add are a bonus.

11. Do I need to send you the APK file?

No. We only need the Opt-in Web Link provided by the Google Play Console. Our testers install directly from the Play Store, acting exactly like real users.

12. What Android versions do your testers use?

We maintain a diverse physical device lab spanning Android 8 (Oreo) up to Android 14, covering major manufacturers like Samsung, Google Pixel, Xiaomi, and Motorola.

13. How soon do you start testing after purchase?

Delivery begins within 4 to 6 hours. Once you add our emails to your Play Console tester list, our team accepts the invites and begins the 14-day clock.

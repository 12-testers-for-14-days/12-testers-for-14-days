Fix the "Insufficient Testing" Rejection

If you spent 14 days chasing friends only to receive an automated rejection email from Google, you are not alone. Here is how we fix it.

TL;DR: Why was my app rejected?

Google sends the "Insufficient Testing" rejection when their algorithm detects low engagement. This usually means your testers uninstalled the app early, didn't open it daily, or you failed to provide concrete details in the production access questionnaire. Solution: Run a new 14-day cycle with our guaranteed professional testers.

Common Causes of Rejection

1. The "Install and Forget" Trap
If your friends installed the app on Day 1 and never opened it again, Google's analytics register 0 active sessions. This is an instant flag for insufficient engagement.
2. Emulator and Bot Usage
Using cheap $5 tester panels from Fiverr often results in scripted bots clicking through emulators. Google Play Integrity API catches this easily.
3. Poor Questionnaire Answers
When you apply for production, Google asks what feedback you received. If you answer "The app is great, no bugs", the reviewer assumes no real testing occurred. You must provide specific UI/UX feedback.

The Recovery Process

You cannot appeal an "insufficient testing" rejection. You must start a new 14-day closed test. Here is how our App Recovery Service ensures you pass on the second attempt:

  1. Deep Engagement: Our 12 professionals will interact with your app daily on physical devices.
  2. Actionable Feedback: We will provide actual bugs or UI notes (e.g., "The login button contrast is too low on dark mode") for you to submit to Google.
  3. Questionnaire Guide: We provide a copy-paste template tailored to your app category to ensure your application answers satisfy the reviewers.
